Maze Exploration

Help the character find their way home!

Lives: ❤️❤️❤️
Flashlight Uses: 🔦🔦🔦
Phase: Click to use flashlight!

How to Play:

  • 🔦 Click to use flashlight and reveal the maze (10 seconds each, 3 uses total)
  • 👀 Study the maze carefully during flashlight time
  • 🖱️ After flashlight expires, move your mouse to guide the character
  • 🏠 Reach the house to win!
  • ❤️ Avoid dead ends or you'll lose a life (3 lives total)
