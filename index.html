Maze Exploration

Help the character find their way home!

Lives: ❤️❤️❤️
Status: Click flashlight button!

How to Play:

  • 🔦 Click the flashlight button at the top to reveal the maze (10 seconds, 3 uses total)
  • 👀 Study the maze carefully while the flashlight is active
  • 🖱️ After flashlight expires, move your mouse to guide the character
  • ⚫ Only a small area around the character will be visible
  • 🏠 Reach the house to win!
  • ❤️ Wrong paths lose a life (3 lives total)
